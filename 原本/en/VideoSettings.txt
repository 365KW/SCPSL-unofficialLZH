Dynamic Shadows~Enables or disables all dynamic shadows cast by characters, objects, and light sources.\nDisabling this option may improve performance, but scenes will appear flatter.
Dynamic Shadow Resolution~Determines the quality and sharpness of dynamic shadows. Lower values can improve performance, but shadows may appear pixelated.
Facility Lights~Disables most facility lights and replaces them with flat ambient lighting.\nThis can greatly reduce GPU load, but makes the facility look noticeably worse and less atmospheric.
Resolution~Determines the number of pixels used to render the game on your display.\nLower resolutions can improve performance, but make the image appear less sharp and stretched.
Aspect Ratio~Adjusts the width-to-height proportion of the displayed image. Select a ratio matching your monitor to avoid stretching or unused screen space.\n<color=#FF2A2E>Aspect ratios narrower than 4:3 or wider than 21:9 are not officially supported. Depending on the deviation, they may cause the game and its UI to appear stretched and, in extreme cases, cause some UI elements to become inaccessible.</color>
Display Mode~Controls the application window type.\n• Exclusive Fullscreen: The game has full control over the screen.\n• Borderless Window: Creates a full-screen effect by stretching the window to cover the screen. \n• Windowed: Runs the game in a standard window, like other applications.
Max Frame Rate~Target maximum number of frames rendered per second.
Anti-aliasing~Smooths jagged edges in the rendered image.\n• FXAA has a lower performance cost but may slightly blur the image.\n• SMAA provides higher-quality edge smoothing.
V-Sync~Synchronizes the game's frame rate with your display's refresh rate to prevent visible screen tearing on some hardware. Enabling this option may increase input latency, so only use it if you experience such artifacts.
